# Gabel Distribution

Operations of the exclusive distribution of Gabel poles in Slovakia and the Czech Republic, supported by AI Agents that know the business and help run its processes.

## Language

### Parties

**Distributor**:
AcEcom s.r.o., the company holding the exclusive Gabel distribution for Slovakia and the Czech Republic.
_Avoid_: shop, company, we

**Supplier**:
A business the Distributor buys goods from; primarily Gabel, the family-owned pole manufacturer in Italy.
_Avoid_: vendor, manufacturer

**Customer**:
A consumer who buys from the Distributor at retail (B2C).
_Avoid_: client, buyer

**Reseller**:
A business that buys from the Distributor (B2B), such as a sports shop or ski school.
_Avoid_: B2B partner, B2B customer, dealer

**Partner**:
An organization in a marketing cooperation with the Distributor in which no goods are sold, such as Ski Team Slovakia.
_Avoid_: sponsor, cooperation

**Ambassador**:
An individual sponsored by the Distributor who publishes Gabel-related content on their own social media, often a racer supported through a Partner.
_Avoid_: influencer

### Channels

**Go-Noow**:
The Distributor's own e-shop, built by NextCom and selling mostly Gabel products, through which both Customers and Resellers place Orders.
_Avoid_: GoNoow, the e-shop

### Documents

**Order**:
A purchase by a Customer or Reseller placed through Go-Noow and recorded in Pohoda (*prijatá objednávka*).
_Avoid_: sales order, objednávka

**Invoice**:
An invoice the Distributor issues to a Customer or Reseller for an Order (*vydaná faktúra*).
_Avoid_: issued invoice, sales invoice, faktúra

**Purchase Order**:
The Distributor's order of goods from a Supplier (*vydaná objednávka*).
_Avoid_: supplier order, order to Gabel

**Supplier Invoice**:
An invoice the Distributor receives from a Supplier for a Purchase Order (*prijatá faktúra*).
_Avoid_: received invoice, vendor invoice, Gabel invoice

**Receipt**:
A stock receipt in Pohoda (*príjemka*) recording goods taken into stock against a Supplier Invoice. Never a sales receipt.
_Avoid_: goods receipt note, GRN, prijemka

### Knowledge and automation

**Second Brain**:
The Distributor's curated business knowledge (portfolio, strategy, plans, partnerships, events), kept as notes that reference the original documents.
_Avoid_: knowledge base, docs, memory

**Agent**:
An AI component responsible for one business process. It reads freely and acts through Proposals until a person grants it autonomy for a given action.
_Avoid_: bot, assistant, AI component

**Proposal**:
A change to an outside system (Pohoda, a carrier, a social network) prepared by an Agent and awaiting a person's approval.
_Avoid_: draft, suggestion
